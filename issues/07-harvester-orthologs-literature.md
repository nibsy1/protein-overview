# Harvester: orthologs, evolution & literature

## Summary
Orthology/evolution harvester and literature harvester.

## Details
- Orthology: OrthoDB, OMA, PANTHER, eggNOG — orthologs across the clade, confidence, paralogs/expansions; single-copy vs duplicated ECA3-like genes.
- Literature: PubMed by gene/protein name and species nomenclature; for understudied non-model organisms extend to closest model-organism ortholog (clearly labeled).
- No fabricated citations; every reference resolves.

## Acceptance criteria
- ECA3 harvest lists paralogs ECA1/2/4 and rice OsECA3 with orthology confidence; key papers (PMID 18024560, 18567829, 37228331) present.

Ref: PRD §3, §6; skill Stage 1
