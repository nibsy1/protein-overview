# Experiment designer (wet-lab + in-silico proposals)

## Summary
Attaches 1–3 discriminating experiments per surviving hypothesis.

## Details
- Each entry: method, controls, predicted outcome under this hypothesis vs top alternative, feasibility/cost note.
- Mix wet-lab (complementation, ion phenotyping, localization microscopy, mutant analysis) with in-silico checks (structure-based substrate docking, AlphaFold complex prediction, conservation of key residues).

## Acceptance criteria
- Every ECA3 hypothesis has ≥1 discriminating wet-lab and ≥1 in-silico proposal with controls and predicted outcomes.

Ref: PRD §2
