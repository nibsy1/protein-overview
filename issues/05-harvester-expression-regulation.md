# Harvester: expression & regulation

## Summary
Expression/regulation harvester with the standard three-layer package, clade-routed.

## Details
- Package per protein: (1) developmental/tissue atlas, (2) abiotic/biotic stress + hormone compendia, (3) single-cell resolution where available; plus promoter cis-elements, TF binding/ChIP, co-expression networks.
- Plant routing: AtGenExpress/BAR eFP, Genevestigator, Expression Atlas, single-cell atlases, ATTED-II. Animal/fungal equivalents routed per clade (GTEx/HPA, Bgee, MGI, ZFIN, FlyBase, WormBase, SGD).
- Non-model species: SRA-based RNA-seq or explicit "no expression data exists" finding.

## Acceptance criteria
- For AT1G10130: tissue/developmental expression, stress responses, and cell-type expression summarized with sources; gaps marked, not padded.

Ref: PRD §6
