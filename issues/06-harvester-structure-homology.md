# Harvester: structure & comparative homology

## Summary
Structure harvester plus the full comparative-homology section against experimental structures.

## Details
- Experimental: PDB; predicted: AlphaFold DB (confidence, disordered regions), ModelArchive.
- Comparative homology vs top homologs with experimental structures (e.g. SERCA1a for ECA3): superposition of AlphaFold model onto PDB, conserved catalytic residue mapping (phosphorylation site, ATP-binding, ion-binding sites), function-transfer table with evolutionary distance, phylogenetic placement. Labeled tier [d] unless tested in the organism.

## Acceptance criteria
- ECA3 store contains AF-Q9SY55-F1 assessment plus SERCA1a (1SU4 etc.) comparison with residue-level mapping.
